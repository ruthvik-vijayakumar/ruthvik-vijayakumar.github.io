NabuAI is an intelligent knowledge management platform designed to transform how academic researchers and students interact with digital information. Addressing the widespread problem of fragmented and stagnant knowledge, NabuAI offers a unified, AI-powered system that captures, organizes, and activates content from diverse digital sources. The platform, comprising a web application and a chrome extension, converts passive research into an active, conversational knowledge base. By integrating content capture, smart AI-driven curation, and conversational retrieval, it aims to reduce the significant time wasted on manual organization and help users to transform collected data into actionable insights, thereby enhancing productivity and having deeper engagement with their research.
