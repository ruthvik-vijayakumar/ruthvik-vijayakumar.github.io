NabuAI is an AI-powered knowledge management platform that helps researchers and students capture, organize, and interact with information in one unified system. Through a web app and Chrome extension, it turns fragmented research into an active, conversational knowledge base—reducing time spent on manual organization and enabling faster insights and deeper engagement with research.
