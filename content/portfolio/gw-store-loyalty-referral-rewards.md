A full systems analysis and design project for the George Washington University Campus Store, addressing suboptimal customer engagement (1.82% Instagram engagement vs. 2.6% average) and the absence of any retention strategy. Led requirements gathering through stakeholder interviews and market research, then designed a comprehensive Loyalty, Rewards & Referral System featuring tiered membership (Influencer, Partner, Ambassador), points-based purchasing, voucher management with local business partners, and a referral engine. Delivered UML artifacts (use case, activity, class, sequence, and state diagrams), mobile app wireframes for customers, a POS integration UI for employees, and an admin analytics dashboard for store managers. Expected outcomes include an 8% increase in repeat purchases, 18% reduction in churn, and 37% higher retention when loyalty is linked to referrals.
